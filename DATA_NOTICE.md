# Data notice

The records displayed here are a one-time snapshot of publicly viewable funded-
project records from the University Grants Committee Research Grants Council
(RGC) project search service:

<https://cerg1.ugc.edu.hk/cergprod/scrrm00541.jsp>

The snapshot was captured on 8 October 2026 (Hong Kong time). This is an
independent convenience tool and is not affiliated with, authorized by, or
endorsed by the RGC or UGC. The authoritative and most current information
remains the source website.

For this public edition, labelled investigator email and telephone fields were
removed. Email-like strings embedded in project details were also redacted. Do
not use the remaining information for harassment, profiling, spam, or unrelated
solicitation.

When using this data, identify the original RGC project search service, state
the snapshot date, and distinguish original records from your own analysis.
